TVs and streaming boxes

DeviceWorks?Notes
NVIDIA SHIELD TV and SHIELD TV Pro (2015–2019)YesThe main target, used daily, on SHIELD Experience 9.2.x (Android 11). With a computer, it becomes the real Home.
Chromecast with Google TV, and TVs with Google TVYes, with HomeWatchAndroid 12 or later. Google TV's own launcher stays on, and an optional accessibility service, HomeWatch, sends the Home button to Action Center. Tested on a Google TV set running Android 12 (32-bit).
Other Android TV devices, Android TV 11 or laterShould workUntested. Devices with the stock Android TV launcher are set up the same way as a SHIELD.
Android TV 10 or olderNoAction Center needs Android 11 (API 30) or later.
Amazon Fire TVNoFire TV runs Amazon's Fire OS, not Android TV.
Roku, Apple TV, Samsung and LG TVsNoThey don't run Android apps.

The release APK is built for ARM, 32- and 64-bit, which covers these devices. On Android 13 and later, some TVs need one extra step for HomeWatch: see Install.

Media apps: Plex, Plezy, Kodi, Jellyfin

AppWorks?Notes
Plex Media ServerYes, and neededThe cards, the Plex Guide and library search are built from your Plex library. You sign in with a code at plex.tv/link. Without Plex, the apps, the weather and Settings still work.
Plezy and the Plex appYesPlay opens your choice of the two (Settings › Plex › Play in). From the guide's player, you can carry on in either from where the channel was.
KodiAs an appKodi shows up in the Apps row and All apps like any app. Its library doesn't feed the cards. Kodi doesn't add what you're watching to Android TV's Watch Next list, so it doesn't show up in Continue in your apps either.
Jellyfin and EmbyAs appsThey show up in the Apps row like any app. The cards need a Plex library.

Would you use Action Center with Kodi or Jellyfin as your library? Say so in an issue: it helps decide what comes next.

Streaming apps and YouTube TV

  • Every installed app is in the Apps row, ordered by what you open at this time of day, and in All apps. You can give any app a custom banner.
  • Continue in your apps shows what other apps add to Android TV's Watch Next list, such as Disney+, YouTube, Prime Video and Spotify, and OK opens the exact title. Settings › Channels & apps turns each app on or off.
  • Search lists titles that are on the streaming apps installed on your TV, through Plex's catalog. It needs a Plex sign-in.
  • YouTube TV: its networks appear in the Live row after your guide channels.

Other launchers

Other launchers, such as Projectivy, can stay installed as a fallback. On a SHIELD, set-default-home.ps1 saves your current Home before it changes anything, and offers to turn off another launcher's accessibility “Home watcher”, which would otherwise take Home back. rollback-home.ps1 puts both back.

The guide station's host

The guide station is optional: it gives every TV in the house the same Plex Guide. It runs in Docker, or as a Java 21 process, on an always-on machine.

  • Intel and AMD machines (Synology “+” NAS models, mini PCs, most home servers): yes, with the published linux/amd64 image.
  • ARM machines (Raspberry Pi, ARM NAS models): there's no image yet, and running it with Java there is untested.
